## 1. The months' chapters

**Cut: the months' scarves** (DECISIONS 398's call). A month's chapter is its trail, its ornament and its title; page 3,
which gave its scarf, now gives 100 gifts (`almanac.ts CHAPTER_PAGE3_GIFTS`, more than any page between: 25 + 5 a page).
None of the twelve was ever on sale (the chapters arrive from `ROUND8_START`, 2026-12), so no save should hold one, but
each keeps its id and its look under a new source, `{ kind: 'kept' }` (garage.ts): never sold, never found, shown on a
shelf only to a rider who holds it, worn as before, as round 8's chapter paints are kept. `chapterSet` is the trail and
the ornament; `CHAPTER_EXTRAS` no longer lists a scarf; a chapter piece bought before its page still pays its price back
(now the trail, page 6, 380 gifts, or the ornament, page 8, 320).

**The months' trails and ornaments: bigger and bolder, each its month.** (Section 3 of this file has what the critics
said of them, and what was cut after.)
- Trails: each about twice the shop's confetti and fewer (the shop's flakes are 0.2 to 0.26 m, thrown at 26 to 42 a
  second; a month's are 0.3 to 0.62 m, at 6 to 26), so a month's trail is a few big shapes where the shop's are many
  small ones: long rays of dawn (First Light), big icicles that drop and burst (The Long Frost), big drops of light
  (Thaw Lanterns), big blossoms (Blossom Run), tall flame tongues (Ash and Ember), big rayed suns (Midsummer Snow), big
  paper bows (Night Market), big paper fans (Paper Cranes), big crescents (Harvest Moon), the hollow lanterns twice the
  size (The Haunting), snowballs twice the size (First Snow); the star strings as they were (Lantern Night).
- Ornaments: every month's a third bigger again (grown about the post's top); the Sun Disc a rising sun in honey gold
  with seven long rays (in cream it read lilac in shade, the critics' "two lilac shapes" with the fan), the Paper Fan
  nine pleats in cream and umber.

## 6. The garage's screens in the round's language (G6, after U4)

The other screens' family test (DECISIONS 399) found the garage's kit out of family: an off-white filled tab (a fourth
button look), body copy at regular weight, a header of mixed pills floating over the scene (Back, the title, the wallet,
the Lantern Row), the title left of a Back where every room says Close, its chips at the top left, a 12 px phone gutter,
and on a phone its tiles below the fold. `src/ui/garage.ts` and `garage.css` were rewritten onto `kit9` (U4's way),
everything else of the garage kept (its model, its acts, its focus keys, its moment):

- **One kit9 sheet** (`gs9-sheet is-right`): at the right of a desk, where the place keeps the rider left of it (the
  scene's 17% offset), from the bottom on a phone held upright (kit9's sheet, at most 56% of the height so the rider
  stands above it). Its head as every room's: "Garage" in italic 900 Title Case, focusable, and a quiet **Close** with
  its word and glyph (the doors' rooms say Close; "Back" was the old garage's). Nothing of the garage floats over the
  scene any more but the moment's one line.
- **The gifts in marigold**, as the results draw them ("1,240 gifts to spend", the glyph and the figure with its word);
  the Lantern Row a small secondary pill beside them, pressed while the camera is in the rafters.
- **The shelves as pills in one track**, the picked one pressed as settings' quality is (a lighter fill and a hairline),
  never a filled off-white tab; arrow keys, Home and End move along them.
- **The shelf's pieces before the piece picked**, in one order on both forms: on a desk a two-column grid in its own
  scrolling band (at most 36% of the sheet, its edges fading as kit9's sheet bodies do), on a phone one row of tiles
  right under the shelves, so the pieces are in sight without scrolling; a picked tile pressed like a tab (no off-white
  ring, which read as a focus ring); a price in marigold with its word; "Wearing", "Yours", "Earned in play", "In its
  month".
- **The piece picked**: its name in italic 900, its line at the system's text weight (700), where it comes from, its one
  action. **One primary at most**: Buy (vermillion, "Buy for 80 Gifts", then "Confirm 80 Gifts") or a tier's Fit;
  Wear It and Ride This Sled secondary; Watch and Listen quiet with their glyphs and words. What a piece waits on is said
  with its figure's word ("580 gifts, 340 more to go"; "Ride 3 more stage lines on it"; "Opens after the deck").
- **A sled's card**: its trait, its action, its signature trick with Watch, then "Its Track" (kit9's section cap) with
  the stage lines ridden and how a tier opens, the five tiers as 18 px cards, "On the Post" and its ornaments as pills.
- **Words**: curly apostrophes in every name and line shown (`words.ts curly`: "Will-o’-Wisp", "Keeper’s Lantern",
  "The Old Run’s set"); the names `translate="no"`; Title Case on headings and buttons; no lone icons.
- **Keys**: Escape (the sheet's own keydown), Backspace and the pad's B (`ui.ts back`, `menus.ts closeTop`) close the
  garage alone and focus returns to the door that opened it, as DECISIONS 398's seams left it; focus lands on the
  heading when it opens (kit9's way), the picked tile a Tab away. The place script's focus keys are kept (`close-garage`,
  `g-tab-*`, `g-tile-*`, `g-act`, `g-watch`, `g-rafters`), so `r9-shop-place.js` still drives it.

web-design-guidelines (the Vercel Web Interface Guidelines, fetched 27 November), read against `garage.ts` and
`garage.css`: fixed a focus ring on the heading where kit9's sheets have none, hover states for the shelves' pills and the
ornament pills, `translate="no"` on the names. Clean: every control has its words (no icon-only button); the live region
is polite; headings step h2, h3, h4; `:focus-visible` rings; transform and opacity only (no `transition: all`), every
duration a token that is zero under reduced motion; `touch-action: manipulation`; `overscroll-behavior: contain` on the
strip and the card; the safe-area insets in the phone sheet's foot; numbers through `Intl` (`fmt`) and tabular; Title
Case on buttons and headings.
