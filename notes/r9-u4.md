## The items carried to this lane

1. **`introsSeen`**: a "New" on a door not yet opened survives a reload. `save.ts` gains two optional fields read by a
   sanitising reader (`loadIntros`): `intros` (what a run opened, by its door and its one line, the newest 8) and
   `introsSeen` (the day's news lines opened, each with its day, the newest 24). `app.ts` reads and writes them in
   place of its session fields: a run's "New" leaves the save when its door is opened; the day's news ("Three new
   contracts today.") is opened for that day, so the same news tomorrow is new again. An old save loads whole (no
   field written); a strange one is read safely (`tests/r9u4.test.ts`).
2. **The Daily and Cup room's count**: "0 of 4 done" over four rows.
3. **A garage piece's glyph on the results**: `RewardCard.shelf` (the Almanac's and the sets' pieces carry it,
   `spineview.ts card`), `almanac.ts rewardGlyph` draws its shelf's glyph; four new glyphs in the game's family
   (`icons.ts`: scarf, trail, ornament, and a brush for a finish), on the 24 px grid with 2 px round strokes.
4. **Escape while the reel plays from Details**: checked first. The results hide while the reel plays (Details closes
   with them), so the window's `back()` found nothing to close and one Escape did only skip the reel; but the results
   then came back at their front with the whole story played again, Details gone. Now `reelui.ts` takes Escape (and
   Backspace) at capture and marks it taken, so `back()` can never act on the same press; and the results, shown again
   after a reel or a clip asked for in Details, come back landed with Details open and focus on Watch Your Run or Save
   Clip (`results.ts`). Seen on a desk (`look3/desk-reel-esc.png`, focus "Watch Your Run").
5. **The mountains' skies**: a mountain's sky now lives beside its ridges in `art.ts` (`skyOf`), drawn as the art's own
   background, so every surface that draws a mountain (the picker's chips, the results' new mountain, an album page and
   its stickers, a toast's disc) follows every biome with no rule of its own; the twelve `.b-<biome>` rules in
   `style.css` and the one in `screens.css` are gone (Lanternfall takes `screens.css`'s, which won the cascade). A look
   the table does not know is an ink dusk, never another mountain's sky. **What each new mountain must add:** its
   `BiomeId` in the contract's `MountainCard.biome` union, and one entry in `art.ts` (its ridges in `RIDGES`, its sky
   in `SKY`). The album finds a mountain's look through the sim's table (`art.ts mountainBiome`), so its stickers get
   their sky with no table of their own.
6. **The Long Night at sixteen legs and the picker at eight**: above.
