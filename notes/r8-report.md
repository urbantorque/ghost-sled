# Round 8: the report

Round 8 is on `main` and live at https://urbantorque.github.io/ghost-sled/ (DECISIONS 387), with its two stretch items,
Trials and Save Clip. Everything below is told at length in DECISIONS 367 to 387,
the README's "Round 8: beauty and the hook", ART.md and the lanes' notes (`notes/r8-*.md`); this is the short
version for the owner. **Nobody has played this build with hands.** A test going green is permission to ask you to
play, nothing more.

## What changed for a player

- **The air is a language, not a spin.** What the steer is doing when you press decides the trick: steer to a side
  and press to grab that side (three depths), steer with it to spin, against it to tweak, centred to flip; let go
  into the landing for a stomp. Eighteen named tricks, one a sled, each with its pose, name and sound; a deep grab's
  warning ring round the sled's shadow; a perfect landing that holds the picture for a breath and throws a ring of
  snow. A short grab held into the snow lands clean; only a deep one on a committed flight is a fall.
- **Charms: a build chosen by steering.** Up to three gates a run, each three arches of light; the one you ride
  through is yours for the run, with no menu and no stop. Twenty-four charms in three families and seven named
  pairs; the middle arch is always the safe survival one. The Almanac widens the pool from six to all 24.
- **Spirit Flow pays and shows.** A level from 0 to 6 built from style that outlives a banked combo: it multiplies
  the lantern's gain and the points, gives a little speed, and is drawn as richer colour, a flame off the scarf, wind
  lines, sparks and brighter ghosts.
- **Rivals you meet on the snow.** Level with one of The Twelve, your grudge or your own best for two seconds and a
  showdown starts to a marker 300 m on; a clean close pass steals a wisp. Pool riders race, draft and duel each
  other, so every ghost does what you can.
- **One rank, an Almanac and reasons to come back.** Every run pays experience into one Rider Rank; fifty Almanac
  pages, the first ten each changing play. A daily flame, three daily contracts, Cup leagues, a Lantern Row to light
  over each finish town, and monthly chapters that never expire. Gifts are still the one currency; nothing bought wins
  a race; no ads.
- **A first hour paced by design.** A first run laid round what a beginner rides, an opener to pass early, one idea at
  a time in the first 15 s, rewards spread over the first ten runs, the first duel on run 3.
- **Results that sell the next run.** A directed five seconds over the live scene: the moment, the nearest rider
  named, the rank bar stopping short of the next page, what opened turned over card by card, and one button chosen
  for the run (Claim, Rematch, Finish the mission, Next Duel, today's Daily, or Run Again with the nearest goal).
- **Somewhere to go after The Twelve.** Mirror mountains (every course turned left for right: picture, steering and
  sound), the Long Night (whole mountains back to back, the lantern, get-ups and last three charms carried leg to
  leg), and a second hour on every mountain (Pinecrest at Dusk, Frostfang at Noon, Ember at Night, Sakura in the
  Evening, Lanternfall at First Light), each an Almanac reward picked under Ride. And a **Trial** a week (from
  Almanac page 35): a twist on a named mountain (mirrored, a mood, the other hour or an event, one charm family, and
  what counts: the clock, the clock without a get-up, style points or perfect landings), the same for everyone, with
  bronze, silver and gold set by the week's own field; picked on the Cup's card.
- **Photo mode and album frames.** Pause or the results' camera holds the run; turn the lens round the rider, save a
  PNG with or without a run card. Album stickers are frames from the run that earned them. **Save Clip** on the results
  saves the run's reel as a short video (about 15 s, its three best shots, the game's sound, the run card at its end);
  a second tap on a phone hands it to the share sheet.
- **The look.** Snow with three surfaces, glints and relief the light draws on High; openings composed past the
  trench with a low lens beside the rider; Ember lit from the lava's side; the great lantern lit over the rider at
  Lanternfall's finish; lamps that light the air; speed in the world (near crystals, posts and poles strobing past,
  blur only at the frame's edge, chatter on hard snow); every action an event (a perfect's hold and shockwave, a
  dash's white-gold ignition, a ward's shards, a passed ghost's flare, a finish scaled to the result); trick poses on
  ghosts too; a stable image on High and Ultra (the frame rebuilt from the ones before it, the rider drawn sharp over
  it); and the critic loop's fixes on all five mountains (DECISIONS 383).

## What only scripts have judged

- Every bar above is a pilot's, not a person's: the lab's pilots press the button the way a script presses it.
  "A near goal exists" and "the button names it" are counted, not felt.
- Every look score is a fresh model critic's, told only ART.md and shown stills or strips. A new critic moved frames
  nothing had touched by up to a point and a half, so a pass's mean is a band; what held is that a fault named and
  fixed was not named again. None of them is a player at speed.
- The trick map was tuned so a rider using all of it scores 1.67 times a spin-only one; whether a person finds the
  grabs, or grabs by accident when they meant a spin and flip, is unknown (a hint teaches "press, then steer").

## What needs a person

- **"Reads faster."** The speed lane's world-speed cues were judged a better picture on every pair; whether the game
  *feels* faster at the same speed cannot be judged from stills.
- **The get-up.** A fall is round 7's; the flow and events lanes changed what surrounds it. Does it still feel fair?
- **A duel by a person.** The Twelve's duels are won by the lab's pilots; no person has tried the first three.
- **The hero frame.** The best frame of each mountain at speed, on your own screen.
- **Low on a real phone or a cheap laptop.** Everything Low was measured on is this laptop throttled six times.
- **The flame on the scarf** (7 of 12 stills read as fire, DECISIONS 377) and **the snow's relief** (7 of 10 on High,
  380): both merged short of their bars, for your eye in motion.
- **The other hours** (381): judged short against ART.md's day by their critics, shipped as Almanac rewards for your
  eye.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Low at 6x against round 7** (DECISIONS 382, 384). Not met: round 8 is 4 to 10 fps under round 7 on every
   mountain (above). Round 8's field rides beside you, so a Low frame on Ember draws about two more ghosts than round
   7's did; two lanes cut everything that did not change the picture, and the critic loop added nothing measurable.
   Going further on Low means showing less of the field (fewer ghosts in glass, say). The call is yours: restate Low's
   bar as round 7's plus the field, or carry it into round 9.
2. **The expert's race** is live 0.67 of a run (round 7 0.70): a fast rider pulls away from the field on Ember and
   Sakura. The mid and stunt pilots' races are more live than round 7's. Leave it, or let the field's pacer reach
   further for an expert (a sim change, SIM_VERSION 11).
3. **The flame on the scarf (7 of 12) and the snow's relief (7 of 10)** shipped short of their bars. Keep them, tune
   them after you have seen them in motion, or take them out.
4. **The other hours** shipped as Almanac rewards though their critics judged them short against ART.md's day. Keep
   them as rewards, or hold them back until a later round lifts them.
5. **Saving on CrazyGames.** Save Photo and Save Clip are browser downloads (a clip's second tap uses the share sheet);
   whether CrazyGames' frame allows either is untested, and its SDK shares no image or video. Try both on the portal,
   desk and phone; if neither works there, hide them on that platform or ask CrazyGames (DECISIONS 385). A rewarded
   placement, its leaderboard and a friends feature are yours to decide; none was added.
6. **The camera.** Every mountain's worst frames are the camera's: through a leap's lip for a slow rider, the opening
   and the First Drop seen from the big-air lens, a flip read as a fall, the rider a speck over the biggest leaps, the
   phone's lower third empty. A camera lane is the largest look lift left.
7. **Ghosts in the sky.** Pool riders' recorded lines sometimes put a ghost high over a town or at the lens. The sim
   is closed, so a fix is in how a ghost is drawn (held to the snow, faded high), not where it is.
8. **Geometry, not paint.** Frostfang's span and crevasse walls, Lanternfall's Cloud Sea, Ember's slots and Sakura's
   terraces want built forms; the review lanes reached what palettes and dressing could.

## The fifteen-minute route

Run `npm run dev` and open the address it prints (`?debug` is on in dev, which the shortcuts below need). A private
window is a fresh save.

1. **A first run (3 minutes), fresh save, Pinecrest, High.** Ride as a new player would. Is the first minute one idea
   at a time? Do you pass the opener early, meet the first charm gate, and know which arch you took? At the results:
   does the one button name something you want?
2. **The air (3 minutes), same save, Pinecrest again.** On a jump, steer to a side and press: a grab. Hold it longer:
   the ring round the shadow warns you. Let go just before the snow: a stomp. Press centred, then steer: a spinning
   flip. Does the rule read without the README? Does a deep grab held into the snow feel like your fault?
3. **The field and a showdown (3 minutes), the same save's third run.** The field is laid across your own runs now.
   Ride level with a rider for two seconds: does the showdown start where you meant it to? Is the race near you most
   of the run? Is the duel on run 3 winnable?
4. **Speed and flow (2 minutes): `?debug&m=2&gates=3&charms=all` (Ember, all three gates, every charm).** Chain tricks
   until the flame lights off the scarf. Does the game read faster than round 7 at the same speed? Does the flame read
   as fire?
5. **After The Twelve (2 minutes): `?debug&m=4&hour=alt` (Lanternfall at First Light), then `?debug&m=0&mirror`
   (mirror Pinecrest), then `?debug&trials` and pick the Trial on the Cup's card.** Do the other hour and the mirror
   feel like somewhere new? Does the Trial's twist read in a second, and is its medal fair?
6. **Photo mode, the clip and the album (2 minutes).** Pause in a good place, press Photo, turn the lens, save with the
   run card. At a results screen press Save Clip: would you send it? Open the album from the title: are the stickers
   frames you remember?
7. **Low (1 minute): `?debug&tier=low&m=2`.** Does Low still look made and run smoothly? On a phone if you have one:
   open the dev server's network address and ride Low.
